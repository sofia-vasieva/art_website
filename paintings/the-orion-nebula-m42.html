The Orion Nebula (M42)

Watercolour · 17 × 17 cm · January 2024

The Orion Nebula (M42).

I usually work on two "identical" paintings simultaneously to make sure I get a good one! But they're never quite identical, and whilst I was happy with both in this case, they have clear differences between them. I felt that the first was more detailed and similar to the original, whilst the second one had a little more depth to it.
