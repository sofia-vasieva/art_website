The Antennae Galaxies (NGC 4038/NGC 4039)

Watercolour · 14.8 × 10.5 cm · October 2024

The Antennae Galaxies (NGC 4038/NGC 4039), in the constellation Corvus, are undergoing a galactic collision.

As the galaxies gradually merge, they are also undergoing a period of rapid star formation.

In a few billion years, it's likely that the Milky Way and our neighbouring galaxy, Andromeda, will undergo a similar collision.
