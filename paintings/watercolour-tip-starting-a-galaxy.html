Watercolour tip: starting a galaxy

Watercolour

Watercolour tip #1: when paining a galaxy or any kind of glowing space object, start by painting a sort of glowing region without worrying at all about the specific shape of the object, or any fine details. This is the beginning of an M82 painting (an early attempt). Detail such as the red "explody bits" are layered on top layer on.
