The Southern Pinwheel Galaxy (M83) II

Watercolour on paper · 10.5 × 14.8 cm · October 2026

Another watercolour interpretation of M83, the Southern Pinwheel Galaxy, because I enjoyed painting the first version so much and wanted to try looking at this object in a different way.

M83 is a massive, grand-design spiral galaxy near the constellation border of Hydra and Centaurus. It is at the centre of the M83 galaxy group, next to the Centaurus A galaxy group, and the two are sometimes classified as one larger group.
